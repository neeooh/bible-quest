## World English Bible (WEB) Topics and selected passages

A collection of 54 Bible passages across ten topics, with a short, plain-language explanation of each passage.

> **Note:** The quotations below follow the WEB wording used in the collection. For multi-verse references, some quotations are selected excerpts rather than necessarily reproducing every word of the full passage. Check the cited reference in a WEB Bible before publishing or using the text for memorisation.

## 1. Faith

*Trusting God, believing in His promises and relying on Him rather than our own understanding.*

### Proverbs 3:5–6

> Trust in Yahweh with all your heart, and don't lean on your own understanding. In all your ways acknowledge him, and he will make your paths straight.

**Explainer:** Trust God even when you cannot see the whole picture. Let your faith guide your decisions instead of relying only on your own judgement.

### Hebrews 11:1

> Now faith is assurance of things hoped for, proof of things not seen.

**Explainer:** Faith means trusting in what God has promised, even when you cannot yet see the outcome.

### Mark 11:22–24

> Jesus answered them, "Have faith in God. For most certainly I tell you, whoever may tell this mountain, 'Be taken up and cast into the sea,' and doesn't doubt in his heart, but believes that what he says is happening, he shall have whatever he says."

**Explainer:** Jesus teaches the importance of trusting God and praying with faith. The image of moving a mountain shows how powerful genuine faith can be.

### Romans 10:17

> So faith comes by hearing, and hearing by the word of God.

**Explainer:** Faith grows as you listen to, read and reflect on God's word.

### 2 Corinthians 5:7

> For we walk by faith, not by sight.

**Explainer:** You do not need to understand or see everything before you trust God and keep moving forward.

### Hebrews 11:6

> Without faith it is impossible to be well pleasing to him, for he who comes to God must believe that he exists, and that he is a rewarder of those who seek him.

**Explainer:** A relationship with God begins with believing in Him and sincerely seeking Him.

## 2. Anxiety & Fear

*Finding peace, overcoming fear and trusting God during difficult and uncertain times.*

### Philippians 4:6–7

> In nothing be anxious, but in everything, by prayer and petition with thanksgiving, let your requests be made known to God. And the peace of God, which surpasses all understanding, will guard your hearts and your thoughts in Christ Jesus.

**Explainer:** Bring your worries to God through prayer instead of carrying them alone. His peace can steady your heart even when your circumstances remain difficult.

### Matthew 6:25–27

> Therefore I tell you, don't be anxious for your life: what you will eat, or what you will drink; nor yet for your body, what you will wear. Isn't life more than food, and the body more than clothing?

**Explainer:** Jesus reminds us not to let worry about everyday needs take over our lives. God knows what we need, and constant anxiety cannot add anything to our lives.

### Isaiah 41:10

> Don't you be afraid, for I am with you. Don't be dismayed, for I am your God. I will strengthen you. Yes, I will help you. Yes, I will uphold you with the right hand of my righteousness.

**Explainer:** God promises His presence and support in frightening or uncertain situations. You do not have to face hardship relying only on your own strength.

### Psalm 55:22

> Cast your burden on Yahweh, and he will sustain you. He will never allow the righteous to be moved.

**Explainer:** Give your worries and burdens to God. The verse encourages you to trust that He will sustain you through difficult times.

### John 14:27

> Peace I leave with you. My peace I give to you; not as the world gives, I give to you. Don't let your heart be troubled, neither let it be fearful.

**Explainer:** Jesus offers a deeper peace than the temporary comfort the world can provide. His words encourage you not to let fear rule your heart.

### Psalm 34:4

> I sought Yahweh, and he answered me, and delivered me from all my fears.

**Explainer:** The psalmist describes turning to God in fear and finding help. It encourages you to seek God honestly when you feel afraid.

## 3. Love

*Understanding God's love and learning to love others with kindness, patience and compassion.*

### 1 Corinthians 13:4–7

> Love is patient and is kind; love doesn't envy. Love doesn't brag, is not proud, doesn't behave itself inappropriately, doesn't seek its own way, is not provoked, takes no account of evil; doesn't rejoice in unrighteousness, but rejoices with the truth; bears all things, believes all things, hopes all things, endures all things.

**Explainer:** This passage describes what genuine love looks like in everyday behaviour. Love is patient, kind, humble and committed to what is good.

### 1 John 4:19

> We love him, because he first loved us.

**Explainer:** Our ability to love God comes in response to His love for us. His love is the starting point, not something we have to earn.

### John 15:12–13

> This is my commandment, that you love one another, even as I have loved you. Greater love has no one than this, that someone lay down his life for his friends.

**Explainer:** Jesus calls us to love others with the same selflessness He showed. Real love is expressed through care, sacrifice and action.

### 1 John 4:8

> He who doesn't love doesn't know God, for God is love.

**Explainer:** Love is central to God's nature. A life with God should therefore be reflected in the way we treat other people.

### John 13:34–35

> A new commandment I give to you, that you love one another, just as I have loved you, you also love one another. By this everyone will know that you are my disciples, if you have love for one another.

**Explainer:** Jesus says that love should be one of the clearest signs of following Him. Faith should be visible in how we treat others.

### Romans 12:10

> In love of the brothers be tenderly affectionate to one another; in honor prefer one another.

**Explainer:** Treat others with genuine affection and respect. Instead of always seeking recognition, look for ways to honour and value them.

## 4. Wisdom

*Seeking God's guidance, making better decisions and learning to distinguish right from wrong.*

### James 1:5

> But if any of you lacks wisdom, let him ask of God, who gives to all liberally and without reproach; and it will be given to him.

**Explainer:** You can ask God for wisdom when you do not know what to do. He welcomes sincere requests for guidance.

### Proverbs 16:3

> Commit your deeds to Yahweh, and your plans shall succeed.

**Explainer:** Bring your plans and actions before God. Let your decisions be shaped by your trust in Him rather than by ambition alone.

### Proverbs 4:7

> Wisdom is supreme. Get wisdom. Yes, though it costs all your possessions, get understanding.

**Explainer:** Wisdom is worth pursuing and should be treated as a priority. Learning to understand what is right matters more than material wealth.

### Psalm 119:105

> Your word is a lamp to my feet, and a light for my path.

**Explainer:** God's word can guide your next step, even when you cannot see the entire road ahead.

### Proverbs 15:22

> Where there is no counsel, plans fail; but in a multitude of counselors they are established.

**Explainer:** Wise decisions often involve listening to trustworthy advice. You do not have to work everything out alone.

## 5. Forgiveness

*Learning to forgive others, letting go of resentment and seeking forgiveness for our own mistakes.*

### Ephesians 4:31–32

> Let all bitterness, wrath, anger, outcry, and slander, be put away from you, with all malice. And be kind to one another, tenderhearted, forgiving each other, just as God also in Christ forgave you.

**Explainer:** Let go of bitterness and treat others with kindness and compassion. Forgiveness is rooted in the forgiveness God has shown us.

### Matthew 6:14–15

> For if you forgive men their trespasses, your heavenly Father will also forgive you. But if you don't forgive men their trespasses, neither will your Father forgive your trespasses.

**Explainer:** Jesus teaches that receiving God's forgiveness should change how we respond when others wrong us. Forgiveness is not something to take lightly.

### Colossians 3:13

> Bearing with one another, and forgiving each other, if any man has a complaint against any; even as Christ forgave you, so you also do.

**Explainer:** Relationships involve mistakes and frustration. This verse calls us to be patient and forgive others as Christ has forgiven us.

### 1 John 1:9

> If we confess our sins, he is faithful and righteous to forgive us the sins, and to cleanse us from all unrighteousness.

**Explainer:** When you honestly admit your wrongdoing to God, you can trust His promise to forgive and cleanse you.

### Luke 6:37

> Don't judge, and you won't be judged. Don't condemn, and you won't be condemned. Set free, and you will be set free.

**Explainer:** Jesus warns against being harsh and condemning towards others. Be willing to show the mercy and grace you hope to receive.

## 6. Strength

*Finding courage, perseverance and strength through God when facing hardship and adversity.*

### Philippians 4:13

> I can do all things through Christ, who strengthens me.

**Explainer:** Christ gives strength to endure changing circumstances, including both hardship and abundance. This is about relying on Him, not assuming every ambition will succeed.

### Isaiah 40:29–31

> He gives power to the weak. He increases the strength of him who has no might. Even the youths faint and get weary, and the young men utterly fall; but those who wait for Yahweh will renew their strength. They will mount up with wings like eagles. They will run, and not be weary. They will walk, and not faint.

**Explainer:** Even strong people become tired and discouraged. Those who hope in God can find renewed strength to keep going.

### Joshua 1:9

> Haven't I commanded you? Be strong and courageous. Don't be afraid. Don't be dismayed, for Yahweh your God is with you wherever you go.

**Explainer:** Courage does not mean having no fear. It means moving forward with confidence that God is with you.

### Psalm 46:1

> God is our refuge and strength, a very present help in trouble.

**Explainer:** God is a safe place to turn when life becomes overwhelming. His help is not distant or unavailable in times of trouble.

### 2 Corinthians 12:9

> He has said to me, "My grace is sufficient for you, for my power is made perfect in weakness." Most gladly therefore I will rather glory in my weaknesses, that the power of Christ may rest on me.

**Explainer:** You do not have to hide your weaknesses from God. His strength can be at work in you even when you feel unable to cope on your own.

## 7. Hope

*Remembering God's promises, finding encouragement and maintaining hope even when life feels difficult.*

### Romans 8:28

> We know that all things work together for good for those who love God, for those who are called according to his purpose.

**Explainer:** God can work even painful and confusing experiences towards His good purposes. This does not mean that everything that happens is itself good.

### Jeremiah 29:11

> For I know the thoughts that I think toward you, says Yahweh, thoughts of peace, and not of evil, to give you hope and a future.

**Explainer:** God reassures His people that their suffering and exile are not the end of their story. The promise was originally spoken to the Israelites in exile, but it still offers a picture of God's faithfulness.

### Romans 15:13

> Now may the God of hope fill you with all joy and peace in believing, that you may abound in hope in the power of the Holy Spirit.

**Explainer:** Hope grows through trusting God. This verse is a prayer that God will fill you with joy, peace and lasting hope.

### Lamentations 3:22–23

> It is because of Yahweh's loving kindnesses that we are not consumed, because his compassion doesn't fail. They are new every morning. Great is your faithfulness.

**Explainer:** Even in the middle of suffering, God's mercy remains. Each new day is a reminder of His compassion and faithfulness.

### Psalm 42:11

> Why are you in despair, my soul? Why are you disturbed within me? Hope in God! For I shall still praise him, my Savior and my God.

**Explainer:** The writer honestly acknowledges inner pain while reminding himself to keep hoping in God. Faith does not require pretending that everything is fine.

### Isaiah 43:2

> When you pass through the waters, I will be with you; and through the rivers, they will not overflow you. When you walk through the fire, you will not be burned, and flame will not scorch you.

**Explainer:** God promises to remain with His people through overwhelming trials. The passage offers reassurance that hardship does not mean abandonment.

## 8. Marriage & Relationships

*Building healthy relationships based on commitment, mutual respect, faithfulness and selfless love.*

### Ephesians 5:25

> Husbands, love your wives, even as Christ also loved the assembly, and gave himself up for it.

**Explainer:** Husbands are called to love their wives through selfless care and sacrifice, following Christ's example rather than acting selfishly or domineeringly.

### Colossians 3:12–14

> Put on therefore, as God's chosen ones, holy and beloved, a heart of compassion, kindness, lowliness, humility, and perseverance; bearing with one another, and forgiving each other, if any man has a complaint against any; even as Christ forgave you, so you also do. Above all these things, walk in love, which is the bond of perfection.

**Explainer:** Healthy relationships need compassion, humility, patience and forgiveness. Love brings these qualities together.

### Genesis 2:24

> Therefore a man will leave his father and his mother, and will join with his wife, and they will be one flesh.

**Explainer:** Marriage is presented as a deep and committed union in which two people build a shared life together.

### Ecclesiastes 4:9–10

> Two are better than one, because they have a good reward for their labor. For if they fall, the one will lift up his fellow; but woe to him who is alone when he falls, and doesn't have another to lift him up.

**Explainer:** A good relationship involves mutual support. Partners should help each other through difficulties rather than leaving one another to struggle alone.

### 1 Peter 4:8

> And above all things be earnest in your love among yourselves, for love covers a multitude of sins.

**Explainer:** Deep love makes room for patience and forgiveness. It does not mean ignoring harm or excusing wrongdoing, but choosing not to hold every mistake against someone.

## 9. Loneliness

*Finding comfort in God's presence, overcoming isolation and remembering that you are not abandoned.*

### Deuteronomy 31:8

> Yahweh himself is who goes before you. He will be with you. He will not fail you nor forsake you. Don't be afraid. Don't be discouraged.

**Explainer:** God promises to go with His people and not abandon them. His presence can bring courage when the future feels uncertain.

### Psalm 27:10

> When my father and my mother forsake me, then Yahweh will take me up.

**Explainer:** Even if the people closest to you let you down, God remains a source of care and belonging.

### Psalm 23:4

> Even though I walk through the valley of the shadow of death, I will fear no evil, for you are with me. Your rod and your staff, they comfort me.

**Explainer:** God is present even in the darkest and loneliest parts of life. The image of a shepherd reminds us of His protection and care.

### Matthew 28:20

> Teaching them to observe all things that I commanded you. Behold, I am with you always, even to the end of the age.

**Explainer:** Jesus promises His followers that they will not be left alone. His presence remains with them through every stage of life.

### Psalm 68:6

> God sets the lonely in families. He brings out the prisoners with singing, but the rebellious dwell in a sun-scorched land.

**Explainer:** God cares about people who are isolated and without a place to belong. The verse presents Him as one who brings people into community.

*Finding hope during emotional suffering, remembering God's compassion and finding strength when life feels overwhelming.*

### Psalm 34:18

> Yahweh is near to those who have a broken heart, and saves those who have a crushed spirit.

**Explainer:** God does not turn away from people who are emotionally broken. He is near to those who are hurting.

### Psalm 42:5

> Why are you in despair, my soul? Why are you disturbed within me? Hope in God! For I shall still praise him for the saving help of his presence.

**Explainer:** The writer recognises his own despair instead of hiding it. He speaks to himself with honesty and encourages himself to keep hoping in God.

### Matthew 11:28–30

> Come to me, all you who labor and are heavily burdened, and I will give you rest. Take my yoke upon you, and learn from me, for I am gentle and humble in heart; and you will find rest for your souls. For my yoke is easy, and my burden is light.

**Explainer:** Jesus invites exhausted and burdened people to come to Him. He offers gentleness, rest and a different way of carrying life's difficulties.

### 1 Kings 19:4–5

> But he himself went a day's journey into the wilderness, and came and sat down under a juniper tree. Then he requested for himself that he might die, and said, "It is enough. Now, O Yahweh, take away my life; for I am not better than my fathers." He lay down and slept under the juniper tree; and behold, an angel touched him, and said to him, "Arise and eat!"

**Explainer:** The prophet Elijah reaches a point of deep despair and exhaustion. God's immediate response includes allowing him to rest and providing food, showing care for his physical needs as well as his spiritual life.

### 2 Corinthians 1:3–4

> Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, the Father of mercies and God of all comfort; who comforts us in all our affliction, that we may be able to comfort those who are in any affliction, through the comfort with which we ourselves are comforted by God.

**Explainer:** God offers comfort in times of suffering. The comfort we receive can also help us show compassion to others who are going through difficult experiences.

## A note on the passages

Some passages appear in more than one topic in spirit, because biblical themes overlap. The collection keeps each reference under its most relevant category rather than repeating the same passage unnecessarily.

These passages can offer spiritual encouragement, but they are not a substitute for practical support, medical care or mental-health treatment. In particular, depression is not a failure of faith.


# Business, Career & Purpose

Bible passages about building a business, developing a career, working diligently, making wise decisions, managing money, and pursuing meaningful goals.

**Translation:** World English Bible (WEB)

## 1. Business, Entrepreneurship & Building Something

### Proverbs 16:3

> Commit your deeds to Yahweh, and your plans shall succeed.

**Explainer:** Bring your work and ambitions before God. Make thoughtful plans, do your part, and keep your priorities grounded in something bigger than personal success.

### Proverbs 16:9

> A man's heart plans his course, but Yahweh directs his steps.

**Explainer:** It is sensible to set goals and choose a direction, while accepting that life may take you somewhere you did not expect. Stay willing to adapt.

### Proverbs 21:5

> The plans of the diligent surely lead to profit; and everyone who is hasty surely rushes to poverty.

**Explainer:** Sustainable progress usually comes from careful planning and consistent effort, not impulsive decisions or the hope of a quick win.

### Luke 14:28

> For which of you, desiring to build a tower, doesn't first sit down and count the cost, to see if he has enough to complete it?

**Explainer:** Before taking on a major project, understand what it will require. Estimate the costs, consider the risks, and make sure you have a realistic plan.

### James 4:13–15

> Come now, you who say, "Today or tomorrow let's go into this city, and spend a year there, trade, and make a profit." Whereas you don't know what your life will be like tomorrow. For what is your life? For you are a vapor that appears for a little time, and then vanishes away. For you ought to say, "If the Lord wills, we will both live, and do this or that."

**Explainer:** Planning and ambition are not the problem; assuming you control the future is. Make plans with humility, remembering that life is uncertain and your plans may change.

## 2. Hard Work, Discipline & Persistence

### Colossians 3:23–24

> And whatever you do, work heartily, as for the Lord, and not for men, knowing that from the Lord you will receive the reward of the inheritance; for you serve the Lord Christ.

**Explainer:** Give your work genuine care and effort, even when nobody is watching. Let the quality of your work reflect your values, not just the desire to impress someone.

### Proverbs 14:23

> In all hard work there is profit, but the talk of the lips leads only to poverty.

**Explainer:** Ideas and plans matter, but they have to lead to action. Progress comes from doing the work, not endlessly talking about what you intend to do.

### Proverbs 22:29

> Do you see a man skilled in his work? He will serve kings. He won't serve obscure men.

**Explainer:** Take your craft seriously and keep improving your skills. Excellence can create opportunities and open doors that ordinary effort may not.

### Ecclesiastes 9:10

> Whatever your hand finds to do, do it with your might; for there is no work, nor plan, nor knowledge, nor wisdom, in Sheol, where you are going.

**Explainer:** Give your present work your best effort while you have the opportunity. Do not put off meaningful action indefinitely.

### Galatians 6:9

> Let us not be weary in doing good, for we will reap in due season, if we don't give up.

**Explainer:** Good work can take time to produce results. Keep going when progress is slow, while remembering that persistence is not the same as refusing to learn or change direction.

## 3. Wisdom, Decision-Making & Strategy

### Proverbs 15:22

> Where there is no counsel, plans fail; but in a multitude of counselors they are established.

**Explainer:** Do not make every important decision alone. Seek advice from people with sound judgement and relevant experience, then weigh their counsel carefully.

### Proverbs 24:3–4

> Through wisdom a house is built; by understanding it is established; by knowledge the rooms are filled with all rare and beautiful treasure.

**Explainer:** Lasting things are built through wisdom, understanding, and knowledge. A strong business or career needs more than enthusiasm; it needs good judgement and a solid foundation.

### Proverbs 27:23–24

> Know well the state of your flocks, and pay attention to your herds: for riches are not forever, nor does even the crown endure to all generations.

**Explainer:** Pay attention to the details of what you are responsible for. Understand your resources, customers, finances, and operations rather than assuming everything is going well.

### Proverbs 18:15

> The heart of the discerning gets knowledge. The ear of the wise seeks knowledge.

**Explainer:** Keep learning. Wise people do not assume they already know enough; they actively look for information that can improve their decisions.

## 4. Financial Wisdom, Wealth & Money

### Deuteronomy 8:18

> But you shall remember Yahweh your God, for it is he who gives you power to get wealth; that he may establish his covenant which he swore to your fathers, as it is today.

**Explainer:** Remember that your abilities and opportunities are not reasons for arrogance. Be grateful for what enables you to earn and build, and do not let success make you forget your values.

### Proverbs 13:11

> Wealth gained dishonestly dwindles away, but he who gathers by hand makes it grow.

**Explainer:** Wealth built through honest, steady effort is more dependable than money gained through dishonest shortcuts. Focus on creating value over time.

### Proverbs 11:1

> A false balance is an abomination to Yahweh, but accurate weights are his delight.

**Explainer:** Be fair and honest in business. Do not deceive customers, manipulate prices, or misrepresent what you are selling. Integrity matters even when dishonesty might seem profitable.

### 1 Timothy 6:10

> For the love of money is a root of all kinds of evil. Some have been led astray from the faith in their greed, and have pierced themselves through with many sorrows.

**Explainer:** Money itself is not presented as evil; making it the thing you love above all else is the danger. Do not sacrifice your character, relationships, or peace just to accumulate wealth.

## 5. Courage, Ambition & Trusting God

### Joshua 1:9

> Haven't I commanded you? Be strong and courageous. Don't be afraid, neither be dismayed: for Yahweh your God is with you wherever you go.

**Explainer:** Moving into something unfamiliar can be frightening. This passage encourages courage and perseverance in the face of uncertainty, trusting that you are not alone.

### Matthew 25:21

> His lord said to him, "Well done, good and faithful servant! You have been faithful over a few things, I will set you over many things. Enter into the joy of your lord."

**Explainer:** In the Parable of the Talents, the servant is praised for being faithful with what was entrusted to him. The passage encourages responsible use of your abilities and opportunities rather than burying them through fear or inaction.

## A Balanced Biblical Perspective on Success

The Bible encourages diligence, wisdom, responsible stewardship, courage, and the development of one's abilities. These principles can apply naturally to building a business or pursuing a career.

At the same time, it warns against greed, dishonesty, pride, and making wealth the purpose of life. Financial success is not proof that God favours someone, and faith does not guarantee that every venture will succeed.

A useful way to approach work and ambition is to:

- Develop your abilities and use them well.

- Work diligently rather than only dreaming about success.

- Plan carefully and seek wise advice.

- Treat customers, colleagues, and competitors honestly.

- Be courageous when facing uncertainty.

- Stay humble and remember that money is a tool, not the purpose of life.

Lust & Sexual Self-Control

Overcoming lust, resisting sexual temptation, practising self-control and keeping your thoughts and actions pure.

1. Matthew 5:27–28

You have heard that it was said, ‘You shall not commit adultery;’ but I tell you that everyone who gazes at a woman to lust after her has committed adultery with her already in his heart.

Explainer: Jesus teaches that faithfulness begins in the heart, not just in physical actions. What we deliberately entertain in our thoughts matters.

2. 1 Corinthians 6:18–20

Flee sexual immorality! Every sin that a man does is outside the body, but he who commits sexual immorality sins against his own body. Or don’t you know that your body is a temple of the Holy Spirit who is in you, whom you have from God? You are not your own, for you were bought with a price. Therefore glorify God in your body and in your spirit, which are God’s.

Explainer: Your body deserves respect and should not be treated as an instrument for satisfying every desire. Paul encourages us to honour God through our physical choices.

3. 1 Thessalonians 4:3–5

For this is the will of God: your sanctification, that you abstain from sexual immorality, that each one of you know how to control his own body in sanctification and honour, not in the passion of lust, even as the Gentiles who don’t know God.

Explainer: God calls people to practise sexual self-control rather than being ruled by uncontrolled desires. Self-discipline is part of living a life of faith.

4. 2 Timothy 2:22

Flee from youthful lusts; but pursue righteousness, faith, love, and peace with those who call on the Lord out of a pure heart.

Explainer: Don’t simply try to resist temptation. Replace unhealthy desires with positive pursuits such as faith, love, righteousness and meaningful relationships.

5. Galatians 5:16

But I say, walk by the Spirit, and you won’t fulfill the lust of the flesh.

Explainer: Focus on living according to God’s guidance rather than giving in to every physical desire. Your actions should be guided by your values, not temporary impulses.

6. Colossians 3:5

Put to death therefore your members which are on the earth: sexual immorality, uncleanness, depraved passion, evil desire, and covetousness, which is idolatry.

Explainer: Don’t allow destructive desires to control your life. Paul encourages actively rejecting behaviours and cravings that pull you away from God.

7. Job 31:1

I made a covenant with my eyes, how then should I look lustfully at a young woman?

Explainer: Job describes making a conscious commitment to control what he looks at. It’s a reminder that self-control often begins with the choices we make about what we expose ourselves to.

8. 1 Corinthians 10:13

No temptation has taken you except such as is common to man. God is faithful, who will not allow you to be tempted above what you are able, but will with the temptation also make the way of escape, that you may be able to endure it.

Explainer: Everyone experiences temptation, and experiencing it doesn’t mean you’ve failed. God provides opportunities to resist it and choose a different path.
